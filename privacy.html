Kascout Analytics — Privacy policy

Kascout Analytics is a private football scouting tool. One coach uses it on their own computers and phone. There are no accounts with us, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app can access in your Google account

  • Its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (permission drive.appdata). The app keeps one file there: the synced copy of your scouting library. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files.
  • Your email address (permissions openid and email). It is only shown in the app so you can see which account is linked.

What is stored, and where

  • Your scouting library is stored on your own devices: on your computers, and on your phone in the browser's storage. A synced copy is kept in the hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • The library holds teams, seasons, opponents, games, imported plays, live charts, game notes, reports, templates and settings. Player evaluations are not synced.
  • The app talks only to Google. It sends nothing to the app's makers or to anyone else. There are no analytics, ads or third-party services.
  • On Windows, your Google sign-in is stored encrypted on that computer. On the phone, a one-hour Google sign-in token is kept in the browser.

Removing your data

  • From the app: on a computer, open Backup & Sync → Google sync → Sign out of Google. On the phone, open Sync → Remove data from this phone.
  • From Google Drive: open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Kascout Analytics → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove the app's access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data

Kascout Analytics' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to sync your own scouting library between your own devices. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
